# HOLLOWMAW: Update 1 — THE DEEPER DARK

You got out through the lighthouse gate. Behind you, the ground cracked open.

## New: the Crawlers
- Pale, eyeless, dog-sized things that drop off the Warden's back when it starts to hunt.
- **They are blind and hunt by sound.** A careless slap of your hand is what brings them.
- **They walk on walls and ceilings** and squeeze into vents, so vents are no longer completely safe.
- **They latch onto your hand.** That hand can't grab anything until you **shake it off with three fast swings**.
- Every second one clings, it **shrieks**, and the Warden hears it.
- Your flashlight makes them flinch away, but holding the beam on them drains the battery faster.
- **Arachnophobia mode** (Settings) turns them into soft glowing blobs that behave exactly the same.

## New tools
- **Throwables.** Bottles, tin cans and stones lie around the levels. Grip to pick one up, swing and let go to throw. Wherever it lands, things go to listen. Bottles shatter.
- **Hiding spots.** Lockers, wardrobes, barrels, crawlspaces, and in one place something worse.
  - Climb in and the door pulls shut. The world goes muffled and your heartbeat gets loud.
  - Keep your hands still, or you'll be heard.
  - The Warden can't see you in there, unless it watched you climb in. It may come and open the door anyway.
  - Some hiding spots have a back way out.
- **Flares.** Rare. Shake one to strike it for 20 seconds of red light. Crawlers won't come near it, and the Warden comes to look, so throw it somewhere useful.

## Three new levels
Finish The Escape and a hole opens in the Playground.

**6 — The Nest.** The Warden sleeps in a cavern on a heap of old beds and chairs. Steal the three glowing stones beside it. Listen to its breathing: it speeds up as it starts to wake.

**7 — The Flooded Chapel.** The water rises every minute. It's slow, it's slippery, and you can't swim. Climb the drowned pews, the pillars, the organ pipes and the bell tower while the Warden wades the nave.

**8 — The Heart.** Breathing walls and a heartbeat you can feel. Grip the cords and pull until they snap, while the Warden and a swarm of Crawlers hunt you. Then climb toward the light. There is a true ending.

## The Warden learned some things
- **Slam.** If you're out of reach on old scaffolding or a beam, it brings both fists down on it.
- **Peek.** It puts its eye to windows and gaps, and holds it there.
- **Memory.** It patrols more often where it caught you last time.

## New modes
- **Nightmare** (after the true ending): the Hollow Warden, which is darker, jawless, longer-fingered and faster, with a glowing chest. Sharper senses, half the lanterns, and no batteries.
- **Endless Descent** (after the first ending): randomly assembled floors of tunnels, rooms, bridges, vent mazes, shafts and caverns. Find the pit and go deeper; every floor brings more Crawlers and a faster Warden, and the music gets heavier. Being caught ends the run. Your best floor is shown on the menu.
- **Time Trial** (any level you've finished): no Warden, a timer on your wrist, and ghost hands replaying your best run.

## Collect, read, wear
- **9 carved toys** (one in each level) unlock things to wear at the **Shelf** in the Playground: five fur patterns, a bell bracelet, a lantern-style flashlight and a crown. There's a mirror next to the Shelf.
- **18 lore notes**, folded into paper planes, two in every level. Read them all to learn who the Warden was. Found notes can be re-read from the menu.
- **14 achievements**, shown on a board in the Playground. Can you finish a level without ever being hunted?

## Settings and comfort
- **Grab mode:** hold to carry, or toggle.
- **Subtitles** for important sounds, with direction (e.g. "[heavy footsteps — left]").
- The menu board has new pages: Modes, Shelf, Notes and Achievements. Locked items show how to unlock them.

## Fixes and under the hood
- Your old save is migrated automatically: unlocked levels, toys and settings are kept.
- Locomotion is unchanged.
- Everything is still generated in code: no new downloads, still a single file.
